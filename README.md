# The Art of Business Dining

> **A Student's Guide to Professional Dining Etiquette**  
> *Created by Darwin Sara — Confidence Starts at the Table.*

---

## Welcome

Welcome to **The Art of Business Dining**, an educational website designed to help university students develop the confidence and professional skills needed to navigate business dining situations.

Whether attending a networking dinner, meeting potential employers, or participating in a professional lunch, understanding dining etiquette can make a lasting impression.

---

## About This Project

This website provides practical guidance on business dining etiquette, professional communication, table manners, and networking skills. Its purpose is to help students feel comfortable, confident, and prepared for professional dining environments.

---

## What You'll Learn

- **The Basics:** Understand business dining etiquette and its importance in professional environments.
- **Before the Dinner:** Learn about appropriate attire, punctuality, preparation, and reservations.
- **Table Etiquette:** Discover proper table manners, napkin etiquette, utensil usage, and dining behavior.
- **Conversation & Networking:** Develop professional communication skills and learn how to build meaningful connections.
- **Test Your Knowledge:** Challenge yourself with an interactive quiz covering essential business dining practices.
- **Student Learning Survey:** Reflect on what you learned and evaluate your confidence in professional dining situations.

---

## Interactive Learning

Explore an eight-question interactive quiz designed to reinforce the concepts presented throughout the website.

The website also includes a student learning survey featuring questions about business dining etiquette, professional behavior, and the knowledge gained from exploring this guide.

---

## Learning Objectives

By exploring this website, students will be able to:
1. Understand the importance of professional dining etiquette.
2. Demonstrate appropriate table manners.
3. Identify professional and inappropriate dining behaviors.
4. Communicate confidently during business meals.
5. Apply dining etiquette principles in real-world professional situations.

## Assignment Requirements

This website was developed to address the requirements of the Business Dining Etiquette assignment.

The following features are included:
- **Internal Links:** Navigation between the homepage and educational sections, including the quiz, student survey, and references.
- **External Links:** Links to the official University of Arizona website and relevant professional dining etiquette resources.
- **Graphic Features:** Editorial photography, visual elements, and a table-setting infographic to support the educational content.
- **Advanced Feature:** A functional eight-question interactive quiz with answer validation, scoring, progress tracking, and feedback.
- **Student Survey:** All eight required student-learning survey questions are presented on the website.
- **Website Design:** A responsive, organized, and visually consistent layout designed for desktop and mobile devices.
- **References:** A dedicated section containing the external sources used to research the educational content.
- **Spelling and Grammar:** Website content is presented in English with attention to clarity, readability, and professional communication.
